## The measurement problem

[VISION.md](../00-company/VISION.md) commits to being "the most trusted"
system, and [Open Questions](../01-problem-space/open-questions.md) asks
"How should trust be measured?" Those two facts together mean the central
commitment currently has no definition of success.

## Trust versus privacy

They are related but not identical, and the sources sometimes merge them.
Privacy concerns what the system holds; trust concerns whether the user can
see, predict, and correct what it does. Privacy material lives in
[10-privacy](../10-privacy/README.md).
