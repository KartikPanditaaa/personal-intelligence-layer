## Evidence status

Nearly everything in this section is undecided by design.
[21-financial-projections.md](21-financial-projections.md) records that no
costs, pricing, runway, or projections were discussed with numerical values,
and [decisions-log.md](../16-decisions/decisions-log.md) states pricing
direction is "Not yet defined".

Do not introduce a number into this section without a citation. See
[DOCUMENT_STANDARDS.md § 8](../18-templates/DOCUMENT_STANDARDS.md).

## The one decided thing

The business model should align with trust, privacy, personalization, and
long-term value rather than commodity AI features
([20-business-model.md](20-business-model.md)). That constrains pricing design:
a model that monetises usage volume would sit awkwardly against
[Anti-Goals](../00-company/ANTI_GOALS.md), which excludes making users
dependent. That tension is unexplored.
