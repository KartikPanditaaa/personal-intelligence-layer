# 00 Executive Summary

## Status

**Decided:** The project is in the research and planning phase. No
implementation has begun.

## Problem

**Decided:** Existing AI products lose context, require repeated
prompting, fragment work across tools, and often make users more
dependent instead of more capable.

## Proposed Solution

**Decided:** Build a Personal Intelligence Layer spanning writing,
coding, planning, research, and knowledge work.

The long-term direction discussed includes: - persistent memory -
personalization - privacy-first architecture - trust and transparency -
human capability growth - voice and text interaction

## Wedge

**Decided** - Trust - Memory - Human growth - Personalization - Privacy

**Reasoned** The initial visible product may be narrower than the
platform because users buy solutions rather than platforms.

## Target User

**Decided** - Developers - Vibe coders - Knowledge workers

**Reasoned** Corporate users are a likely expansion target because
privacy concerns were discussed extensively.

## Current Stage

Research → Product principles → Architecture planning → Prototype
planning.

Everything beyond this requires founder decisions.
