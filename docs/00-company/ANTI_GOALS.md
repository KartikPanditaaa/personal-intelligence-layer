# Anti-Goals

What this project has explicitly said it will not be or do.

> **Assembly notice.** Every entry is quoted from an existing rejection or
> non-goal and cited to its source. Nothing has been added to this list, and
> nothing has been removed from the sources.

Anti-goals are load-bearing. They are what makes the positive direction mean
anything. Adding to this list is a founder decision; so is removing from it.

## Category anti-goals

**Status: Rejected** — per
[Positioning § Explicitly Rejected Positioning](04-positioning-and-differentiation.md)
and [Decisions Log § Positioning discussions](../16-decisions/decisions-log.md).

| We will not build | Why it was rejected |
|---|---|
| Another autocomplete | The wedge is trust, memory, human growth, personalization, privacy — not completion quality |
| Another AI IDE | Superseded direction; see [Graveyard](../19-graveyard/README.md) |
| Another chat wrapper | No differentiation surrounding the model |

## Competitive anti-goal

**Status: Decided** — per
[Positioning § Competitive Philosophy](04-positioning-and-differentiation.md).

We do not compete on foundation model quality. We compete on the experience
surrounding foundation models.

Restated in [Competitive Analysis](../02-research/16-competitive-analysis.md):
compete on trust, personalization, memory, privacy, and human capability
"rather than raw model quality".

## Experience anti-goals

**Status: Decided** — the negative form of statements in
[Vision and Thesis](01-vision-and-thesis.md) and
[Problem Deep Dive](../01-problem-space/02-problem-deep-dive.md).

| We will not | Source statement |
|---|---|
| Make users dependent on AI | "avoid creating dependence"; "avoid making users dependent on AI" |
| Optimize for delegation over user growth | Existing assistants "optimize for delegation instead of human growth" — listed as a problem to solve |
| Produce generic-sounding output | "AI-generated writing feels generic" — listed as a problem to solve |
| Require users to repeatedly re-explain themselves | "Users repeatedly explain projects and preferences" — listed as a problem to solve |
| Fragment work across tools | "Developers constantly switch between tools" — listed as a problem to solve |
| Encourage cognitive offloading | Listed as a pain point in [Problem Deep Dive](../01-problem-space/02-problem-deep-dive.md) |
