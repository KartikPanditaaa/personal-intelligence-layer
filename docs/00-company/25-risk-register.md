# 25 Risk Register

## Risks Discussed

### Decided

-   Privacy concerns
-   Enterprise trust
-   Context loss
-   AI dependency
-   Feature creep
-   Building too broadly too early

### Reasoned

Severity and likelihood require formal scoring.

## Mitigations Discussed

-   Research-first approach
-   Product constitution
-   Trust-first design
-   User-controlled memory
-   Red-team reviews

Risk ownership and prioritization.
