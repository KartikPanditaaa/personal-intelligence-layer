# 01 Vision and Thesis

## Long-term Vision

**Decided**

Build the most trusted personal intelligence system for knowledge
workers.

The product should: - learn with users - help users grow - preserve
context - avoid creating dependence

## Product Philosophy

**Decided**

AI should augment rather than replace humans.

Automation should remove repetitive work while strengthening long-term
capability.

## Why Now

**Reasoned**

Discussion identified: - rapid AI adoption - increasing dissatisfaction
with generic AI outputs - fragmented AI tooling - demand for trusted
assistants

## Why This Founder

**Requires founder decision**

The thread establishes motivation and technical interest but no formal
founder narrative was agreed.

## Guiding Principle

**Decided**

The product should co-evolve with the user.
