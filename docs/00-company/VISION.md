# Vision

> **Assembly notice.** This document introduces nothing new. Every statement
> below already exists in a source document and is cited to it. Where the
> sources are silent, this document says so rather than filling the gap.
> The detailed originals remain authoritative:
> [Vision and Thesis](01-vision-and-thesis.md) and
> [Executive Summary](00-executive-summary.md).

## The vision

**Status: Decided**

Build the most trusted personal intelligence system for knowledge workers.

*Source: [Vision and Thesis § Long-term Vision](01-vision-and-thesis.md)*

## What the product must do

**Status: Decided**

- Learn with users
- Help users grow
- Preserve context
- Avoid creating dependence

*Source: [Vision and Thesis § Long-term Vision](01-vision-and-thesis.md)*

## Guiding principle

**Status: Decided**

The product should co-evolve with the user.

*Source: [Vision and Thesis § Guiding Principle](01-vision-and-thesis.md);
also recorded in [Decisions Log](../16-decisions/decisions-log.md)*

## Product philosophy

**Status: Decided**

AI should augment rather than replace humans. Automation should remove
repetitive work while strengthening long-term capability.

*Source: [Vision and Thesis § Product Philosophy](01-vision-and-thesis.md)*

## Scope of the vision

**Status: Decided**

A Personal Intelligence Layer spanning writing, coding, planning, research, and
knowledge work.

The long-term direction includes persistent memory, personalization,
privacy-first architecture, trust and transparency, human capability growth,
and voice and text interaction.

*Source: [Executive Summary § Proposed Solution](00-executive-summary.md)*

## Wedge

**Status: Decided**

Trust · Memory · Human growth · Personalization · Privacy

**Status: Proposed** *(recorded as "Reasoned" in the source)*

The initial visible product may be narrower than the platform, because users
buy solutions rather than platforms.

*Source: [Executive Summary § Wedge](00-executive-summary.md)*

## Why now

**Status: Proposed** *(recorded as "Reasoned" in the source)*

Discussion identified rapid AI adoption, increasing dissatisfaction with
generic AI outputs, fragmented AI tooling, and demand for trusted assistants.

*Source: [Vision and Thesis § Why Now](01-vision-and-thesis.md)*

## Current stage

**Status: Decided**

Research → Product principles → Architecture planning → Prototype planning.

*Source: [Executive Summary § Current Stage](00-executive-summary.md)*
