## Blocking dependency

Every document here is blocked on the same unanswered question:
[Open Questions](../01-problem-space/open-questions.md) asks "What is the first
beachhead market?" and "What should the first product actually be?"

[15-market-sizing.md](15-market-sizing.md) lists five candidate markets without
choosing one. [17-go-to-market-plan.md](17-go-to-market-plan.md) lists three
candidate early audiences without choosing one. Until the beachhead is decided,
this section cannot progress past enumeration.
