## Overlap note

[16-competitive-analysis.md](16-competitive-analysis.md) and
[competitive-landscape.md](competitive-landscape.md) cover the same 16
competitors from two different origins (`blueprint/` and `context/`). Both are

question — see the [Documentation Roadmap](../DOCUMENTATION_ROADMAP.md).

## Naming convention

| Kind | Pattern | Template |
|---|---|---|
| Research note | `RES-NNNN-short-slug.md` | [RESEARCH_TEMPLATE.md](../18-templates/RESEARCH_TEMPLATE.md) |
| Customer interview | `interviews/INT-NNNN-persona-YYYY-MM-DD.md` | [CUSTOMER_INTERVIEW_TEMPLATE.md](../18-templates/CUSTOMER_INTERVIEW_TEMPLATE.md) |

Numbers are sequential and never reused.

## The rule for this section

Evidence and interpretation never share a heading. A research note that mixes
what a source said with what we think it means cannot be re-evaluated later
when the conclusion is challenged.

No `RES-` notes and no interviews exist yet. The section contains competitive
