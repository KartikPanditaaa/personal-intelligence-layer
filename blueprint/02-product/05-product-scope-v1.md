# 05 Product Scope V1

## MVP Scope

### Decided

-   Research-first foundation
-   Personalization concepts
-   Long-term memory concepts
-   Human-centered assistance
-   Writing and coding assistance
-   Trust and privacy as core principles

### Reasoned

-   Initial product should likely be narrower than the long-term
    platform.

## Explicit Non-goals

### Decided

-   Building every capability in v1
-   Final production architecture
-   Enterprise rollout
-   Full autonomous AI

## Requires founder decision

-   Exact MVP feature list
-   First platform (desktop/browser/IDE)
-   Voice support in v1
