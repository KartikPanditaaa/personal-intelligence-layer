# Product Brief

## Core idea

Build a personal intelligence layer that spans writing, coding,
planning, and knowledge work. The product should learn with the user
over time, prioritize trust, privacy, and human capability, and avoid
making users dependent on AI.

## Problem it solves

-   Explicitly discussed:
    -   AI tools lose context.
    -   Users repeatedly explain projects and preferences.
    -   AI-generated writing feels generic.
    -   Developers constantly switch between tools.
    -   Existing assistants optimize for delegation instead of human
        growth.
-   Current workarounds:
    -   ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, Copilot, copy/paste workflows, prompt
        engineering.

## Target user/customer

### Explicitly discussed

-   Solo developers
-   "Vibe coders"
-   Knowledge workers
-   Corporate employees
-   Product managers
-   Students \### Assumed during discussion
